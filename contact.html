HOTELS & TOUR COMPANIES

Where to Stay & Who to Book With

Here are five afforable hotels in Bandar Seri Begawan and three ways to book a tour. prices are "from" prices per night for two adults and change with the season, so always check the latest rate on the booking page.

Budget Friendly Hotels

Badi'ah Hotel

About US$40-55 per night

  • Facilities: Outdoor pool, free Wi-fi, fitness center, airport transfer, family rooms
  • From the airport: About 6-8 KM (around 8 minutes by car). Free airport shuttle during limited hours
  • Nearest attractions: Kampong Ayer (a few minute walk) and Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque (about 1.5km)
Check price & book →

The Brunei Hotel

About US$80 per night

  • Facilities: Free Wi-fi, family room, airport transfer, breakfast
  • From the airport: About 10 KM (around 15-20 minutes by car).
  • Nearest attractions: Kampong Ayer and Tamu Kianggeh market
Check price & book →

Higher Hotel

About US$20-35 per night

  • Facilities: Free Wi-fi, Kitchen, breakfast, family rooms
  • From the airport: About 8 KM (around 10 minutes by car).
  • Nearest attractions: Royal Regalia Museum and Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque
Check price & book →

The Capital Residence Suites

About US$50 per night

  • Facilities: Free Wi-fi, free parking, bathtub, airport transfer, breakfast
  • From the airport: 8 KM (around 10 minutes by car).
  • Nearest attractions: Kampong Ayer (a few minute walk) and Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque (about 1.5km)

Times Hotel Brunei

About US$50 per night

  • Facilities: Free Wi-fi, free parking, bathtub, airport transfer, breakfast
